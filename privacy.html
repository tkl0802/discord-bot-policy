Privacy Policy

For 自由的肥鱼 (ZiYou) · Effective date: 11 November 2026

This Policy explains how 自由的肥鱼 (ZiYou) (“the Bot”), operated by 自由, handles information when you use its chat and interactive features on Discord.

1. Information processed by the Bot

When you interact with the Bot, it may receive and temporarily process information made available through Discord, such as your Discord user ID, username or display name, server/channel context, and the message or command you send. This is used to identify the interaction, provide the requested feature, and generate a reply.

2. Chat history and storage by the operator

The operator does not intentionally maintain a persistent database of users' chat messages or chat transcripts. Message content is processed to provide a reply and is not intentionally saved by the operator as a long-term chat history. This describes the operator's Bot system only; it is not a promise that Discord, hosting infrastructure, or third-party services do not retain data in their own systems or logs.

3. DeepSeek AI processing

When you use an AI-powered feature, the message content and context needed to generate a response are transmitted to DeepSeek. DeepSeek independently processes information under its own policies and may collect, use, and retain inputs and related technical information. DeepSeek's current Privacy Policy states that personal data may be processed and stored in the People's Republic of China and retained as necessary for its services and other stated purposes. Review its policy before using the Bot: DeepSeek Privacy Policy.

The operator cannot control DeepSeek's independent retention, storage location, or processing practices. Do not send passwords, payment information, sensitive personal data, or confidential information to the Bot.

4. How information is used

The Bot processes information as needed to respond to messages or commands, provide chat features, maintain service security, troubleshoot errors, and prevent abuse.

5. Sharing

The operator does not sell users' personal information. Information is transmitted to DeepSeek when an AI reply is requested. Discord and the Bot's hosting provider may also process information as part of delivering and operating the service, under their own policies.

6. Security

The operator takes reasonable steps appropriate to a small community Bot to limit access to information. No internet service or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices and requests

You can stop interacting with the Bot at any time. For questions or requests about information handled by the operator, contact the operator through the Discord account/server where the Bot is provided. Requests about information held independently by Discord or DeepSeek must be directed to those providers.

8. Children's privacy

The Bot is not designed to collect personal information from children. Do not submit another person's personal information without an appropriate basis or permission.

9. Changes to this Policy

This Policy may be updated when the Bot's features or data practices change. The effective date will be revised when changes are made.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the operator through the Discord account/server where the Bot is provided.
